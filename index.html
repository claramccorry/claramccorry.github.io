About

I'm an undergraduate physics student at the University of Galway, with a strong interest in radiation physics, medical physics, and biophotonics — the physics of how light interacts with living tissue.

Through the Tyndall Summer Fellowship, I researched upconversion nanoparticles and their potential use in deep-tissue imaging. Outside the lab, I volunteer with Cell Explorers, which has shaped how I communicate and present science, and how I work within a team.
